City Life

Life simulation · Android

Start with a rented flat and a little cash. Get a job, work your way up or open your own firm, raise a child, buy a house and the car you always wanted — and decide what kind of life it adds up to.

What it is

A city you live in, not just drive through. Walk its streets, ride a taxi or a danfo, or drive your own car through real traffic with lights, police and fines. Every building you need is there: the bank, the hospital, the school, shops, the car dealer, the estate agent, the office towers, and an airport at the edge of town.

Apply for jobs and sit the interview. Work your way up, or register your own firm, sit in the CEO’s chair and take on clients. When your child arrives, do the school run and be there when the school day ends — or don’t, and live with it. Take a holiday when the stress gets too much. Everything you choose moves your wealth, your happiness and your stress.

Money is Naira. You earn it, bank it, and spend it; you can also top it up with an optional purchase, or by watching an advert. Nothing in the city is locked behind a payment.

Support

Something wrong, lost progress, or a question about your account or a purchase? Write to support@vibkings.com. Tell us which phone you play on and, if you have an account, the email you signed in with — it makes the answer much faster.

Your account and your data

You can play the whole game without an account; your game is saved on your phone. If you make one, it exists so your life in the city survives a new phone.

Deleting your account

In the game: open the phone, then Account ▸ Delete account. Your account and its cloud save are deleted at once. Or email support@vibkings.com from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted; we do it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Section 7 of the Privacy Policy has the detail.
