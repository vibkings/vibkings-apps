Discover trusted businesses across Africa. Find shops, suppliers, service providers, wholesalers and retailers — every listing here runs on Vibkings Suite.

What it is

Real products from real shops. Browse by category — retail, wholesale, electronics, building materials, fashion, food & beverage, cosmetics, pharmacy, supermarkets and services — or search for a product by name across every business on the Hub.

  • A real storefront for every business — its logo, description, opening hours and photo gallery.
  • Find the actual products — items with a photo show up on the listing and in search across the whole Hub.
  • Reach the business directly — one tap to call or WhatsApp, with no app to install and no sign-up needed.
  • Shop online — add to cart, check out, and track your order.

For businesses

Every business on Vibkings Suite gets a public profile on the Hub automatically — a free storefront, turned on with a toggle in Settings, with no extra setup and no extra cost.
