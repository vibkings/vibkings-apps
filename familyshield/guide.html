Setup guide

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Family Shield has two apps: Family Shield Parent on your phone, and Family Shield on your child’s phone. Setting up takes about 15 minutes. Have your child’s phone with you, unlocked and charged.

On this page: 1. Your phone · 2. Your child’s phone · 3. The setup checklist · 4. The other parent · 5. A managed phone (stronger protection) · 6. Removing it · 7. If something does not work

1. Your phone: the parent app

  1. Install Family Shield Parent (coming soon to Google Play).
  2. Open it and tap Create an account. Use an email you check: you confirm it with a link, and it is how you sign in on a new phone.
  3. Create your family (one per account), then on the Family tab add each child by name or nickname.
  4. Allow notifications when asked, so SOS and other alerts reach you straight away.

2. Your child’s phone: link it

  1. On your child’s phone, install Family Shield (coming soon to Google Play).
  2. In the parent app, on the Family tab, tap Link a phone next to your child. You get an 8-character code, valid for 10 minutes.
  3. On your child’s phone, open Family Shield, enter the code and tap Link this phone. The phone appears on the parent app’s Home tab within a few seconds.

Family Shield is never hidden: it keeps its icon and a notification on your child’s phone, and shows your child what is shared. Please explain it to them.

Some phones (vivo, TECNO, Infinix, Xiaomi and others) also have their own battery settings. If check-ins are late, open the phone’s Settings, find Family Shield and allow it to run in the background.

4. The other parent or guardian

Each parent uses their own account and sees the same children and alerts.

  1. In the parent app, on the Family tab, under Parents & guardians, tap Invite a parent or guardian, and choose guardian or owner.
  2. Tap Share the code and send it, for example by WhatsApp. It works once, for 24 hours.
  3. The other parent installs Family Shield Parent, taps Join a family on the first screen, enters the code and creates their account (or signs in).

5. A managed phone: stronger protection

In Protected Managed-Device Mode, Family Shield manages the phone with Android’s own device management. It is much harder to get around:

  • Blocked apps really cannot open, and apps over their daily limit are paused.
  • The website filter cannot be switched off, and its VPN and Private DNS settings are locked.
  • During screen time, Wi-Fi can be switched off and the phone can be locked to a Family Shield screen where calls, the emergency number and SOS always work.
  • Family Shield cannot be uninstalled from the phone’s app list.

What it needs:

  • A phone that has been factory reset (Android only allows this on a phone being set up from new). Back up photos, chats and contacts first: a reset erases the phone.
  • Do not add a Google account during the phone’s setup until management is turned on.
  • At the moment, a computer to switch management on. We are working on a QR-code setup that needs no computer. Until then, write to support@vibkings.com and we will guide you through it, or set it up for you.

What it is not: it is not impossible to remove. Anyone with the phone can still factory-reset it from Android’s recovery menu, which removes Family Shield and everything on the phone. It does not let Family Shield turn on the camera or microphone, or read messages.

Leaving managed mode: the family owner opens the phone’s card in the parent app and taps Remove management. The phone keeps its data and goes back to Standard Family Mode.

6. Removing Family Shield

  • Stop a phone sharing: in the parent app, open the phone’s card and tap Remove this device. On a managed phone, tap Remove management first. Then uninstall Family Shield from the phone.
  • Delete your account and data: in the parent app, on Your families, tap Delete account. See the Privacy Policy, section 8.
