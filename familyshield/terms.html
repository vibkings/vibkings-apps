Family Shield helps you look after your child: their phone’s location, SOS and other alerts, screen-time schedules, and app and website rules. It works within what Android allows, and it is honest about it:

  • Rules can be worked around. In Standard Family Mode a person with access to the phone can turn off the filter, remove permissions or uninstall the app; Family Shield reports what it can detect, but cannot prevent this. Protected Managed-Device Mode makes this harder, but not impossible, and the phone can always be reset and recovered.
