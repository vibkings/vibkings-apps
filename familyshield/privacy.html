Vibkings Apps  ·  Family Shield

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Vibkings Family Shield (“Family Shield”) is made by Vibkings Global Limited (“we”, “us”). It has two Android apps: Family Shield Parent, which a parent or guardian installs on their own phone, and Family Shield (the child app), which a parent installs on their child’s phone and links to the family. This policy explains what each app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

The short version: Family Shield is for parents and guardians looking after their own children. It collects only what its safety and screen-time features need, shows it only to the guardians of that family, keeps location for 30 days and websites visited for 14 days, never sells anything, and is never hidden on the child’s phone. It does not read messages, record calls, take photos or turn on the camera or microphone.

1. Who it is for

The parent app is for adults who are a child’s parent or legal guardian. The child app may only be installed on a phone of a child in your care, with your authority as their parent or guardian. It must not be used to monitor another adult, a partner or anyone else, even with their agreement. The child app stays visible on the phone, with its own notification, and tells the child what is shared.

2. What the parent app collects

  • Your account: your email address and a password. The password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored only as a cryptographic hash; we never see it.
  • Your family: the family name you choose, the guardians you invite and their role, and each child’s display name (a nickname is enough).
  • Trusted contacts you add: their name, phone number and relationship to the child. They are shown on the child’s phone so the child can call them, and calls from these numbers can ring through on the child’s phone (section 3).
  • Your rules: websites and apps you block or allow, daily app limits, screen-time schedules, and Places (a name, a point on the map and a size) you add.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for your phone, so alerts reach you.

What happens only on the child’s phone

  • The website filter runs on the phone as a local VPN. It answers website lookups on the phone and blocks the ones guardians chose. Other lookups are sent, encrypted, to Cloudflare’s DNS service to be answered (section 6). The filter only sees website names, never the content of pages.
  • Guardian calls always ring: if this is turned on, Android shows the child app the number of an incoming call. If it is a trusted contact’s number, the app turns the ringer up. The number is checked on the phone and is not sent to us; calls are never blocked, recorded or listened to.

What Family Shield never collects

It does not read SMS or chat messages, does not read the call log, does not record calls or audio, does not take photos or video, does not turn on the camera or microphone, does not read anything typed, and does not collect the child’s contacts, photos or files. It does not use Android’s Accessibility features. It does not show adverts and does not use advertising identifiers.

4. Why we collect it

Only to provide the features the family uses: show guardians where the phone is and whether it is safe and online, deliver SOS and other alerts, apply and report screen-time rules and app and website rules, handle requests for more time, help find a lost phone, and keep the service secure (for example audit records of who changed a rule). We do not sell data, we do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we do not build profiles of children or parents.

5. Who can see what

Everything a child’s phone sends is visible only to the guardians of that family, in the parent app. No other family, and no member of the public, can see it; this is enforced by our database for every request. The child’s phone shows the child what is shared from it. Our staff look at an account’s data only to answer a support or deletion request from that account, or when the law requires it.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions; everything in sections 2 and 3 is stored there. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries the “wake up” messages that ask the child’s phone to check in. They contain no personal data. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Expo push notifications deliver alerts to the parent app. A notification contains the child’s display name and the kind of alert (with an app or Place name where it matters), never a location or message. expo.dev/privacy
  • Cloudflare DNS answers the child phone’s website lookups that the filter does not block (the same kind of lookup every phone makes). cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Play services on the child’s phone provide Android’s geofencing for Places, and Android’s address lookup turns a location into an area name. Google handles these under its own policy. policies.google.com/privacy

Your data may be stored or processed in a country other than your own, including the United States and the European Union.

8. Removing a phone and deleting your data

  • Stop collecting from a phone: in the parent app, open the phone’s card and tap Remove this device. The child’s phone stops sending anything at once. On a phone in Protected Managed-Device Mode, the family owner can first tap Remove management.
  • Delete your account in the app: in the parent app, on the Your families screen, tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. At once, every family you are the only owner of is deleted with everything its linked phones sent, and you are removed from families that have another owner or where you are a guardian (those families stay with the other guardians). Your sign-in is deleted too. Deleting is permanent.
  • Or by email: write to support@vibkings.com from the email address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We do the same within 30 days and confirm when it is done.
  • You can also ask for a copy of the data we hold about your family, or ask us to correct it.

9. Children

The child app collects information about a child only on the instruction of that child’s parent or guardian, who sets it up, can see everything it sends, and can remove it at any time. Guardians are responsible for having the authority to set it up and for explaining it to their child. A child or parent can contact us at any time about a child’s data. Only adults may create an account in the parent app.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each family’s data is separated from every other family’s by our database’s access rules, which are tested automatically. Linked phones use their own credentials, which are withdrawn when a phone is removed. No app holds our server’s privileged keys.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what Family Shield collects, we will update this policy and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will say so in the apps before it takes effect.
