Vibkings Suite

Business management · Web · Installable app

Run your shop from one dashboard — built for your whole team. Inventory, sales, purchases, staff and daily cashflow, all in one place. Built for African small businesses.

What it is

Every sale and stock change is saved safely the moment it happens, so two staff members on two devices never overwrite each other’s work or sell the same last item twice. You can still browse your inventory, invoices and reports with no signal.

  • Inventory that never lies — every stock change is logged, and you can sell by the piece or the carton.
  • Professional invoices — cash, credit, part-payment and exchanges, printed with your shop’s own name and mark.
  • Staff with real permissions — each role sees only what it should, and every action traces to a name.
  • Barcode & expiry — scan stock in and out and track batch expiry, if you need it.
  • Daily closing & cashflow — opening float, till counts, expenses and returns, so you know what should be in the drawer.

Included with every subscription

  • A Business Hub listing — a free public storefront on Vibkings Business Hub that helps new customers find you.
  • Owner Monitor — a lightweight dashboard for owners to check sales, cash and daily closing from their phone, from anywhere.

There is also an optional AI Stock Assistant: a WhatsApp bot for the business owner that answers questions from the shop’s own live data.
