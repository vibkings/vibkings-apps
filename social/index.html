Vibkings Social Manager

Social media management · AI assistant · Web

Your business’s social media, managed in one place. Plan and publish posts, answer customers and spot sales enquiries, with an AI assistant that only uses information your business has approved.

What it does

  • Facebook and Instagram — schedule posts, answer comments and messages in one inbox, and see post results.
  • YouTube — connect your channel to schedule and upload videos, and see their views, likes, comments and watch time.
  • TikTok — schedule videos and see their views, likes, comments, shares and followers.
  • A careful AI assistant — drafts captions and replies from your approved products, prices and policies, and hands anything it isn’t sure about to a person. Nothing goes out without the approval rules you set, and one switch pauses all automation.
  • Your whole team — owners, managers, content creators, support agents and analysts each get the access their role needs.
  • Works with Claude — manage it all by chatting, with the same roles and rules. Add it to Claude.

Early access

Every account connects through the platform’s own official sign-in; we never ask for your passwords. Each platform switches on fully once it finishes that platform’s app review: YouTube is approved, and Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are still in review, so connecting them may be limited until then.

Use it

Open Social Manager

Runs in the browser on any phone or computer. Create an account, set up your business, then connect your pages and channels.

Support

A question about connecting an account, a post that didn’t publish, or your plan? Write to support@vibkings.com and include your business’s name in Social Manager.
