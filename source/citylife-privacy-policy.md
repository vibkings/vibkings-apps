# Privacy Policy — City Life

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

City Life (“the game”) is made by Vibkings Global Limited (“we”,
“us”). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who else can see it, and how to
get it deleted. It covers the Android version of the game and nothing else.

If you only want the short version: **you can play the whole game without
giving us anything.** Your game is saved on your phone. An account is optional, and the only
reason to create one is so your game survives a new phone.

## 1. What the game collects

### If you play as a guest (no account)

- **Your game, on your phone.** Your money, job, home, car, family, settings and the
name you give your character are saved on your own device so the game works offline. None of it is
sent to us.

### If you create an account

Everything above, plus:
- **Your email address**, so you can sign in again on another phone and get your game
back.
- **A password**, which we never see. It is handled by our sign-in provider and stored
as a cryptographic hash, not as text.
- **A copy of your saved game**, backed up to our server while you are signed in (at
most once a minute, and when you leave the app), with the in-game day it was saved on.
- **Your player name**, the name you chose for your character, stored with your
account.

### What the game never collects

We do not ask for and do not collect your real name, your address, your phone number, your
location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone or your camera. The game asks for no special
Android permissions. We do not run our own analytics or tracking, and we do not build a profile of
you.
The radio in the game reads the news aloud with your phone’s own text-to-speech engine. That
happens on your phone; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

## 2. Adverts

The game shows adverts. Most are optional: you choose to watch one for something in return
(Naira for your bank, a hospital bill paid, part of a traffic fine back, a free taxi ride, another
try at a job interview, a hint, a break from stress). There are also occasional full-screen adverts
at natural pauses, such as after sleeping or after a trip; never in your first 30 minutes of play,
and never more than one every 15 minutes. The **Remove ads** purchase turns the
full-screen adverts off.

Adverts are supplied by **Unity LevelPlay** (Unity Technologies) and the advertising
networks it works with. To show adverts and to count them, these companies may collect
**device information and advertising identifiers**, such as your device’s
advertising ID, device model, operating system version, IP address, country and connection type.
This is collected by them, under their own privacy policies, not by us — we never receive
it.

You can limit this on your phone: **Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads ▸**
*Delete advertising ID* (or *Opt out of Ads Personalisation* on older phones).

Unity’s privacy policy:
[unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)

## 3. Purchases

You can buy Naira packs and **Remove ads** in the game. Purchases go through
**Google Play**: Google takes the payment and keeps the record of it, under its own
terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details. The game only receives
Google’s confirmation that a purchase went through, so it can give you what you bought and
restore Remove ads if you reinstall.

## 4. Why we collect it

Only for these reasons:
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Saved game, player name | So your life in the city is there when you come back, and on a new phone |
| Email and password | So you can prove the account is yours and get your game back |
| Purchase confirmations | So you get what you paid for, and so we can help if a purchase goes wrong |
We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

## 5. Who can see what

City Life is a single-player game. Nothing about you is shown to other players: there are no
leaderboards, chat or public profiles. Your email, your saved game and your purchases are visible
only to you and to us.

## 6. Where it is stored

Accounts and backed-up games are stored with **Supabase**, our backend provider, on
servers they operate. Adverts are handled by Unity LevelPlay and purchases by Google Play, as
described above. Depending on where you live, your data may be stored or processed in a country other
than your own, including the United States.
Supabase’s privacy policy: [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

## 7. Deleting your data

You can delete everything we hold about you. Two ways:
- **In the game:** open the phone, then **Account** ▸
**Delete account**. Your account and its backed-up game are deleted from our server at
once. The game on your phone stays; to remove that too, clear the app’s data or uninstall
it.
- **By email:** write to the address in section 11 from the email address on your
account, asking for your data to be deleted. We will delete your account and your backed-up game
within 30 days and confirm when it is done.
Deleting is permanent. A deleted account and its backed-up game cannot be recovered.
You can also ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your account and your backed-up game for as long as the account exists. When you delete
it, it is gone.

## 9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.
If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we will change this policy and update the date at the top. If
the change is significant, we will say so in the game before it takes effect.
