After Dark: Street War

Arcade racing and vehicle combat · Android

Race the rivals, fight the raiders, lose the police, and get home with the money. An arcade racer with a fight in it.

What it is

Night falls and the streets change hands. Six districts, each its own place — towers and cold light downtown, low houses and trees in the old town, factories, market stalls, open highway, and a container port stacked with steel. Six ways to play across sixty levels, plus an endless run with no finish line at all.

The police come in five wanted levels: more cars, then roadblocks, then elite units, then a helicopter overhead. They follow, ram, block the road and call for help — but they never teleport and never appear on top of you. There is always a way out if you can drive.

Five cars, all of them earned. Upgrade the engine, tyres, brakes and nitro, and paint it any of eleven colours. Nothing in the garage is locked behind a payment.
